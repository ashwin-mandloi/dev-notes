Welcome to Dev Notes.

This is my personal technical knowledge base—a place where I document my learning journey as a software engineer. Rather than collecting definitions, I organize my notes around the questions that sparked my curiosity and the answers I discovered through research.

Every topic begins with a question because I believe that meaningful understanding starts with curiosity. These notes capture not only what I learned, but also the thought process that helped me build that understanding.

### A Note to the Reader

>  These notes reflect my current understanding of a topic at the time they were written. They are based on my research, practical experience, and interpretation of the subject. As I continue to learn, my understanding will evolve, and these notes may be revised, expanded, or even corrected.
>  If your understanding, experience, or approach differs from mine, that is both expected and welcome. Software engineering is a field of continuous learning, and there is often more than one valid perspective.

My goal is not to create an authoritative textbook. Instead, I want to build a living knowledge base that I can revisit whenever I need to restart a topic, challenge my assumptions, or deepen my understanding.

If these notes also help someone else think differently, ask better questions, or discover a new perspective, then they have served a purpose beyond my own learning.

>  Curiosity is the starting point. Learning is the journey. Understanding is the destination.
